Field noteJuly 20, 1969 — and onward

Before there were footprints, there was a maybe.

Someone looked at the Moon and wondered what it would feel like to stand there. That question traveled through classrooms, workshops, radio waves, and millions of hopeful minds.

NASA is a collection of those questions made real — rovers that taste the soil of Mars, telescopes that see the first light in the universe, and people brave enough to try.
